Your 90-day strategy

Where you are going, and why this approach fits Andrea's Resort.

Your goal

Increase direct bookings by 50% and grow the social following past 1,000 in the next 90 days.

Andrea's Resort has a perfect 5.0 Google rating, a working online booking system and 37 strong photos on its website. What it does not have is visibility: only 20 reviews, 522 followers across Instagram and Facebook, and one social post every ten weeks. You will get there by posting consistently, building a steady flow of new reviews, and making the resort easy to find in local search.

Your positioning

Most cabin properties in the region compete on price or on the size of the hot tub. Andrea's Resort competes on calm: a quiet stretch of lakefront, cabins that are properly looked after, and owners who answer the phone.

Every post, review reply and web page should sound like the owners talking, and should show the lake.

“The lakeside escape for families and couples who want a peaceful, nature-first getaway with modern comforts and personal hospitality.”

Build social presenceA consistent, engaging social presence that shows the cabin experience and reaches new audiences.
