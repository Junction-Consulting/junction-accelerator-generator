Tactic 1 · Foundation

Social Media Activation

A four-week sprint that takes your social media from dormant to active: a posting rhythm, a month of posts from the photos you already own, short video, and small daily habits.

“The blocker is time, not ideas.”

Part A: set up your content system

1
Fix both profiles before you post anything · about 20 minutes · week 1
  • On Instagram, tap Edit profile and rewrite the bio to say what the resort is and where it is, including the drive time from Kelowna.
  • Add the direct booking link in the Links field.
  • Add four story highlights: Cabins, The Lake, Getting Here, Things To Do.
  • Repeat the bio and booking link on the Facebook page.
2
Set a fixed weekly pattern · week 1
  • Monday is a cabin feature, Wednesday is a local activity, Friday is a guest experience.
  • Block 30 minutes every Sunday to check the week ahead.
  • Write the pattern down and share it with whoever else might post.
Tip. If a week gets away from you, post one photo of the lake with one honest sentence.
