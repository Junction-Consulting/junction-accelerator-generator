Overall grade C

Andrea's Resort has excellent technical foundations, perfect Google ratings, and strong pre-arrival information, but is not fully using social media. Key opportunities include increasing posting frequency and adding operating hours to the website.

Key strengths

  • Perfect 5.0 Google rating with 20 reviews demonstrates exceptional guest satisfaction.
  • Strong technical website foundation with SSL and mobile optimisation.
  • Integrated booking system makes reservations straightforward.

Critical gaps

  • Social media is below recommended levels: 522 total followers and posting 0.1 times a week.
  • Operating hours and the seasonal schedule are missing from the website.
  • Contact information could be more prominent on the homepage.
Bottom line. Andrea's Resort delivers an exceptional on-property experience and strong pre-arrival information. The main opportunities are a limited social media presence and adding operating hours. Consistent posting and contact visibility improvements could increase direct inquiries by 40 to 60% within three months.
