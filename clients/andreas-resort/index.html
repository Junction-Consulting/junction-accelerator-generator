Andrea's Resort attracts outdoor adventure travellers, outdoor recreation enthusiasts, wilderness seekers, and international tourists (especially families and couples seeking peaceful retreats) visiting the Mountain Valley region for multi-day wilderness experiences, typically aged 35-65 with moderate-to-high income seeking authentic nature experiences away from the city

How they find you

Visitors discover Lakeside resorts through 1) Google searches for 'Mountain Valley accommodations', 'Clear Creek fishing lodges', 'mountain resorts' 2) Trip planning for Scenic Highway road trips 3) Fly-fishing forums and outdoor adventure blogs 4) Word-of-mouth from previous guests - digital presence is critical as this is a destination requiring significant advance planning (8-hour drive from major centers)

Where you are going, and why this approach fits Andrea's Resort.

Your goal

Andrea's Resort is a lakeside cabin property with a perfect 5.0 Google rating, a working online booking system and 37 strong photos on its website. What it does not have is visibility. There are only 20 reviews, 522 followers across Instagram and Facebook, and one social post every ten weeks.

Over the next 90 days the goal is to increase direct bookings by 50% and grow the social following past 1,000, by posting consistently, building a steady flow of new reviews, and making the resort easy to find in local search.

Social Media Activation

Build a consistent, engaging presence that drives discovery and turns followers into guests.

Tactic 1 · Foundation
0%

What this is

A four-week sprint that takes your social media from dormant to active. You will set a posting rhythm, turn the photos you already own into a month of posts in one sitting, start publishing short video, and build the small daily habits that grow an audience.

Why it matters

Leisure travellers find places to stay on Instagram and Facebook before they ever search Google. A quiet account suggests a quiet business. Three posts a week for twelve weeks beats ten posts in one burst followed by a month of silence.

What we heard from you

You have beautiful cabin photography and guests who love their stay, but your social accounts do not show it. With 194 followers on Instagram, 328 on Facebook and one post every ten weeks, the accounts look closed. You told us the blocker is time, not ideas.
“The blocker is time, not ideas.”

Tips

  • Post when your audience is online: 7 to 9 in the morning and 7 to 9 in the evening.
