Social Media Activation

Build a consistent, engaging presence that drives discovery and turns followers into guests.

What This Is

A four-week sprint that takes your social media from dormant to active. You will set a posting rhythm, turn the photos you already own into a month of posts in one sitting, start publishing short video, and build the small daily habits that grow an audience.

What This Breaks Into
Set Up Your Content System
Create a Month of Content in One Sitting
Launch Video and Build Engagement
What We Heard From You

You have beautiful cabin photography and guests who love their stay, but your social accounts do not show it. With 194 followers on Instagram, 328 on Facebook and one post every ten weeks, the accounts look closed. You told us the blocker is time, not ideas.

Why It Matters

Leisure travellers find places to stay on Instagram and Facebook before they ever search Google. A quiet account suggests a quiet business. Three posts a week for twelve weeks beats ten posts in one burst followed by a month of silence.

    • Add four story highlights:
    • Cabins
    • The Lake
    • Getting Here
    • Things To Do
    • Repeat the bio and booking link on the Facebook page.

    Set a fixed weekly pattern
    • Monday is a cabin feature, Wednesday is a local activity, Friday is a guest experience.

    • Write the pattern down and share it with whoever else might post. A pattern only one person knows stops when that person is busy.

Make booking direct the easiest option, and stay in touch with guests who have already stayed.

What This Is

Two connected pieces of work: remove the friction from booking on your own website, and start a small, respectful email program for past guests.

What This Breaks Into
Make Booking Direct Easy
Start the Guest Email List

What We Heard From You

Most of your bookings come through third-party sites that take a commission, even from guests who found you by name. You have email addresses for several years of past guests and have never written to them.

Why It Matters

Every booking that moves from a third-party site to your own website keeps the commission in the business. Past guests are the cheapest bookings you will ever get, because they already know the place.

  1. 1
    Put a Book Now button in the site header
