Your visitors and market

Who visits

Andrea's Resort attracts outdoor adventure travellers, outdoor recreation enthusiasts, wilderness seekers, and international tourists (especially families and couples seeking peaceful retreats) visiting the Mountain Valley region for multi-day wilderness experiences, typically aged 35-65 with moderate-to-high income seeking authentic nature experiences away from the city

How they find you

Visitors discover Lakeside resorts through 1) Google searches for 'Mountain Valley accommodations', 'Clear Creek fishing lodges', 'mountain resorts' 2) Trip planning for Scenic Highway road trips 3) Fly-fishing forums and outdoor adventure blogs 4) Word-of-mouth from previous guests - digital presence is critical as this is a destination requiring significant advance planning (8-hour drive from major centers)

Your seasons

Peak season June-September for fishing, hiking, and wildlife viewing - digital strategy should emphasize booking windows 3-6 months in advance, winter months for promoting summer availability, shoulder seasons (May, October) need specific content addressing weather/accessibility. Social content should be year-round to maintain engagement even during closed periods

Where you fit in a trip

Andrea's Resort is a destination anchor in multi-day Mountain Valley itineraries - visitors typically combine with National Forest, Scenic Highway scenic drives, Alpine Meadows, and Clear Creek activities. Digital presence should emphasize 'base camp' positioning and nearby attractions, cross-promote with regional tourism partners, provide itinerary suggestions
