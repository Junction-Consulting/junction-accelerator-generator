Questionnaire Responses

Foothills Quarter Horses · submitted before the onboarding call

This is sample material for a fictional operator. It shows what an operator finds on the Materials page of their dashboard.

Your idea

What experience do you have in mind?

A morning at the ranch where people learn how to handle and ride a horse properly. Not a nose-to-tail trail ride. Something where they leave knowing more than when they arrived.

What do you have that could carry it?

Six steady ranch horses, an outdoor arena, tack for all sizes and about four kilometres of trail on our own land.

Your guest

Who do you picture booking this?

Couples and families with teenagers visiting the Crowsnest Pass for a weekend. Most will have little or no riding experience.

What do visitors ask you for today?

"Can we come see the horses?" We have been saying yes for years and never charging.

Season and capacity

When could you run it, and for how many?

Saturday mornings from late May to the end of September. Six guests at most, one per horse.

Who would you work with?

A cafe in town for the snack, and the local visitor centre to send people our way.

Booking and price

How do people book with you now?

They phone or message us. There is no online booking.

What is your instinct on price?

Somewhere between $100 and $150 a person, but we have never worked out what it costs us to run.

Obstacles and success

What is the biggest thing in your way?

Insurance. We have meant to ask our broker about paying guests and have not done it.

What would success look like a year from now?

A full Saturday most weeks of the summer, and guests telling their friends.
