Coaching Call 1 Summary

Concept and feasibility · Foothills Quarter Horses

This is sample material for a fictional operator. It shows what an operator finds on the Materials page of their dashboard.

What we decided

  • One experience: a half-day horsemanship morning. Groundwork and saddling in the arena, then a guided ride on the ranch trails.
  • The sentence: "A half-day at the ranch where guests learn to read a horse, saddle up properly, and ride the same trails our family has worked for four generations."
  • Guest: weekend visitors with little or no riding experience who want to learn, not only ride.
  • Size and season: up to six guests, Saturday mornings, late May through September.
  • Launch window: first paying departure in the second half of May.

What we ruled out

  • Full-day and overnight versions. They can come later, once the half-day runs smoothly.
  • Guests under 12, for the first season.

What has to be true

  • Commercial liability insurance that covers riding instruction for paying guests.
  • A business licence answer from the municipal district.
  • A written safety briefing and a guest waiver.

Before call 2

  • Work through the Design and Understand Your Market sections of your guide.
  • Call your insurance broker and ask the three questions in the guide.
  • List what one Saturday departure costs you: feed, fuel, snacks and your own hours.
