Coaching Call 2 Summary

Pricing and launch · Foothills Quarter Horses

This is sample material for a fictional operator. It shows what an operator finds on the Materials page of their dashboard.

The number

  • Price: $145 per guest.
  • Cost per departure: worked through in the costing calculator with your own figures, including your time and a margin for the unexpected.
  • Break-even: two guests. That becomes the minimum booking.
  • First-month target: 12 guests, or $1,740 in bookings.

The booking method

  • Online booking through a booking platform, with a link from your own website and social pages.
  • You will compare two platforms on fees and ease of use in Week 6 of your plan and choose one.

The date

  • First departure: Saturday, May 22, 2027.
  • Listing live: by the end of Week 8 of your launch plan, so guests have about four weeks to book.

What has to be true 30 days before

  • Insurance certificate in hand.
  • Waiver reviewed by your broker.
  • Photos taken and uploaded.
  • Saturday dates open for booking through September.

What happens next

Your launch plan is now on your dashboard: an eight-week roadmap with a checklist for each week and a "how to" guide for every step. Choose your start date on the home page to begin.
